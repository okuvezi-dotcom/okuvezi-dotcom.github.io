CannaClub website privacy policy

Effective 10 October 2026 · covers the CannaClub page on this site, the public design mockup and the working demo

In short

The CannaClub web page, the public design mockup and the working demo collect nothing about you. If you ask for a demo, you do it by email, and we use what you send only to reply and arrange the demo.

1. What this policy covers

This policy covers the CannaClub page at https://okuvezi-dotcom.github.io/cannaclub.html the public design mockup at https://okuvezi-dotcom.github.io/cannaclub-mockups/, and the working demo we share by email with club operators and investors who ask for one. It does not cover the CannaClub platform that clubs will use with their members once it goes live. That platform verifies identity and handles orders, so it will have its own, much fuller privacy policy, and a POPIA review, before any club uses it.

2. Who is responsible

CannaClub's website is published by Okuhle Vezi, an individual developer in South Africa, who is the responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) and acts as Information Officer. Contact: ovezi@wamtechsystems.co.za.

3. The CannaClub page

The page has no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising, and loads nothing from other companies: its fonts, images and code are served from this site. Like all of this site, it is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. GitHub's servers receive the technical details every web request carries, such as your IP address and browser type, and may log them for security and operations under GitHub's privacy statement. We don't receive those logs and don't use them. GitHub's servers may be outside South Africa.

4. The design mockup

The mockup is a clickable design, not the real service. Everything in it runs in your browser: the sign-in code it shows, the sample ID number, the clubs, the membership and the delivery are all simulated on your device, and nothing you type is sent to us or saved after you close it. It comes pre-filled with invented details. Please don't type your real ID number or other real personal details into it.

One difference from this page: the mockup loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so when it opens, your browser contacts Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser details under Google's privacy policy. The mockup's photographs are built into the page and are credited inside it.

5. The working demo

The working demo shows the whole platform running: the members' app, the club console, the driver app and the compliance ledger. It is not the real service either. We send its link and access code by email to people who ask for a demo, and it asks search engines not to list it. Every club, member, product, driver and payment in it is sample data: it takes no payments, sends no messages and checks no identity against any real register. Everything runs in your browser, and nothing you type in it is sent to us or to anyone else.

Unlike the mockup, the demo remembers where you got to. It keeps its sample orders, memberships and ledger in your browser's storage on your device, so that it works offline and survives a reload. That data stays on your device until you choose Reset demo in the demo's menu or clear this site's data in your browser (how). Its typefaces, photographs and map are served with it, so it contacts no other company; the photographs are credited inside it. As with the mockup, please don't type your real ID number or other real personal details into it.

6. When you email us

If you write to ask for a demo or with a question, we receive your email address, your name and anything you choose to tell us, such as your company and where you operate. We use it only to reply, arrange and run the demo, and follow up on that conversation. We don't add you to a mailing list, and we don't sell or share your details. We keep the conversation for as long as it is useful to it and no longer than 12 months after our last contact, unless we go on to work together, in which case a separate agreement will say how long records are kept. Our email is handled by our email service provider, whose servers may be outside South Africa.

7. Age

This page is written for businesses and investors. The CannaClub platform is for adults aged 18 and over only.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask whether we hold personal information about you, ask to see it, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to its processing. Email ovezi@wamtechsystems.co.za and we will respond within a reasonable time. You can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa).

9. Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at the top.
