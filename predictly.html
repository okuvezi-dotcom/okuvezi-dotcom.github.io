Predictly is a free prediction game. Points can't be bought, sold, transferred, cashed in or swapped for anything of value. The crowd you trade against in this version is simulated on your phone, and everything you do stays on it. Nothing here is financial or betting advice.

Put it on your phone

It installs from the browser, is under 1 MB, and after the first open it plays with no signal.

iPhone

  1. Open okuvezi-dotcom.github.io/predictly-app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share ⇪, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Predictly from the Home Screen once while you're online. That's when it saves itself to the phone.
  4. After that it works in airplane mode.

Android

  1. Open Predictly in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install when Chrome offers it, or open the ⋮ menu and choose Add to Home screen.
  3. Open it from the home screen once while you're online.
  4. After that it works with no signal.

Questions about Predictly

Is this gambling?

No. You play with free points that have no cash value. You can't buy them, you can't cash them out, and there are no prizes. It's a game about calling what happens next, and it's for adults.

Does it cost anything?

No. There's nothing to buy and there are no ads.

Why does it ask for my phone number?

Its dialling code picks your regional edition, and it signs you in on this phone. No code is sent, no SMS goes out, and the number never leaves your phone. Any number in international format works.

Who am I playing against?

In this version, a simulated crowd of sixteen practice players that lives on your phone. They make calls while you watch, so prices move even in airplane mode. The league table ranks you against them.

Does it really work offline?

Yes. Open it once from your home screen while online and the whole app is saved to the phone: the questions, the crowd, your points and your tickets.

What happens when a question closes?

In this practice version questions don't settle on real-world results. Their closing dates roll forward so there's always something to call, and your points move with the price of what you hold.

Why 18+?

The questions are about money, politics and sport, and the way prices and tickets work looks like betting even though no money is involved. We keep it for adults.
