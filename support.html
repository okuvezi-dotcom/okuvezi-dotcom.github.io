Contact

All three apps are made and published by Okuhle Vezi in South Africa. For help, bug reports, privacy and data requests, accessibility problems or anything else, email ovezi@wamtechsystems.co.za. We read everything and aim to reply within a few working days.

For a CannaClub demo, use this link, which starts the email for you.

Reporting a problem

It helps a lot if your email says:

  • which app, and what you were doing when it went wrong;
  • your phone or computer and its system version (for example "Samsung A15, Android 14" or "iPhone 12, iOS 18");
  • whether you play it in the browser or from the home screen;
  • a screenshot, if you can take one.

If a game won't open offline, open it once from your home screen while you're online and wait a few seconds; that's when it saves itself to the phone.

Deleting your data

Shibobo and Predictly keep everything on your own device and send nothing to us, so there is no account on a server to close: deleting the data on your device deletes all of it. Both apps are served from okuvezi-dotcom.github.io, so clearing that site's data in your browser clears both.

What each app keeps

  • Shibobo: which Skool drills you've finished.
  • Predictly: the phone number you signed in with, your league-table name, language and edition, your points and tickets, and the simulated crowd. Signing out keeps these; follow the steps below to erase them.
  • CannaClub's mockup: nothing; it forgets everything when you close it.
  • CannaClub's working demo: its sample orders, memberships and ledger, until you choose Reset demo in its menu or clear the site's data as below.

On an iPhone or iPad

  1. Touch and hold the app's icon on the Home Screen and choose the option to remove or delete it. A home-screen web app keeps its own storage, and removing it deletes that.
  2. To clear anything Safari itself kept: open Settings → Safari (on iOS 18 and later, Settings → Apps → Safari) → Advanced → Website Data, find okuvezi-dotcom.github.io, swipe left and tap Delete.

On Android (Chrome)

  1. In Chrome, open ⋮ → Settings → Site settings → All sites, tap okuvezi-dotcom.github.io, then Delete data (or Clear & reset).
  2. Touch and hold the app's icon on your home screen and choose Uninstall or Remove.

If you installed from Google Play

The Play versions keep their data inside the app, separate from your browser. Uninstall the app, or open Settings → Apps, choose the app, then Storage → Clear storage. Either removes everything it kept.

If you emailed us

Ask us to delete your emails and any details you sent, at ovezi@wamtechsystems.co.za, and we will.

Accessibility statement

We want these pages and apps to be usable by as many people as possible, and we aim for the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA on this website.

What we've done on this website

  • Pages are built with headings, landmarks and a "skip to content" link, and work with a keyboard alone.
  • Every screenshot has a text description. Text is real text, not pictures of text, and pages still work when zoomed to 200%.
  • The promo films are muted, decorative and never the only place information appears. If your device asks for reduced motion, they don't play on their own.
  • Nothing relies on colour alone: in Predictly, Yes and No always carry the words as well as green and red.

Known limitations in the apps

  • Shibobo is a fast, real-time game. It needs quick touch or controller input and good vision, and it can't be played with a screen reader. Skool lets you practise each control slowly, one at a time.
  • Predictly uses ordinary text and buttons, but it hasn't yet been fully tested with VoiceOver and TalkBack, so some parts, such as the price chart, may be hard to use with them.
  • CannaClub's mockup and working demo are demonstrations and haven't been audited for accessibility.

Tell us

If something on these pages or in the apps is hard to use with your setup, email ovezi@wamtechsystems.co.za with the page or screen, your device and any assistive technology you use. We'll reply and, where we can, fix it.
