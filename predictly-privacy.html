Predictly privacy policy

Effective 10 October 2026 · applies to the free-to-play Predictly app on the web, installed to a home screen, and from Google Play

In short

This version of Predictly runs entirely on your phone. It has no server behind it. What you type and do in it, including the phone number you sign in with, stays on your device. We never receive it.

1. Who is responsible

Predictly is published by Okuhle Vezi, an individual developer in South Africa. For the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA), Okuhle Vezi is the responsible party for Predictly and acts as its Information Officer. Contact: ovezi@wamtechsystems.co.za.

2. What the app keeps on your device

Predictly saves the following in your browser's local storage on your phone, so your game survives a restart and works offline. None of it is sent anywhere.

WhatWhy
The phone number you sign in with, and the country its dialling code points toTo choose your regional edition and to sign you in on this phone. No code or SMS is sent, and the number is never transmitted.
The name you choose for the league table (optional), your language and editionTo show the app the way you set it up.
Your points, your calls and tickets, and price historyThat's the game.
The simulated crowd: its players, wallets and callsSo prices move while you play, even offline. These players are invented.
Whether you've seen the intro and dismissed the install tipSo they don't keep reappearing.

The storage names are predictly.practice.v1, predictly.session and predictly.installHint. Your browser also saves the app's own files so it can run offline; those are the same for everyone.

3. What it doesn't do

  • No accounts on a server, and no syncing between devices.
  • No analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or social-media code, and no tracking cookies.
  • No payments of any kind. There is nothing to buy, so no payment details are ever asked for.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
  • Nobody else sees your name or your calls. The other players in the league table are simulated on your phone.

4. Hosting and downloads (the web version)

The app and this website are hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When your device downloads the app (the first time, and later when it checks for a new version), GitHub's servers receive the technical details every web request carries, such as your IP address and browser type, and may keep them in logs for security and operations under GitHub's privacy statement. We don't receive those logs and don't use them. GitHub's servers may be outside South Africa. Your browser has its own privacy policy, which covers how the browser itself works.

5. Google Play

The Android app on Google Play is this same practice app with all of its files packaged inside it. It runs in your phone's built-in web view, works without a connection, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. Its only permission is Android's standard internet permission, which the practice app doesn't need. Google handles the download and your Google account under Google's own privacy policy, and gives us only summary statistics (such as install counts and crash reports) that do not identify you.

6. Age

Predictly is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children. Because the app sends us nothing, we hold no information about anyone, including children.

7. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask whether we hold personal information about you, ask to see it, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to its processing. For this version of Predictly we hold none, and we will confirm that if you ask. You can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa).

8. Deleting your data

Signing out (Me → Sign out) ends your session on the phone, but keeps your points and tickets in case you sign back in. To erase everything:

  • Installed from the browser: remove Predictly from your home screen, then clear the website data for okuvezi-dotcom.github.io in your browser's settings.
  • From Google Play: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Settings → Apps → Predictly → Storage. Your points, tickets and phone number go with it.

Step-by-step instructions are on the support page. Shibobo is served from the same web address, so clearing that site's data in your browser clears Shibobo's saved progress too.

9. Future versions

A future version may let you play against other people online, which needs a server. If that happens, this policy will be rewritten before that version is released, saying exactly what is sent, why, where it is kept and for how long, and the app will tell you before anything leaves your phone.

10. Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at the top. Questions: ovezi@wamtechsystems.co.za.
