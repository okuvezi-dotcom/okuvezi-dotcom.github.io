Shibobo privacy policy

Effective 10 October 2026 · applies to the Shibobo game on the web, installed to a home screen, and from Google Play

In short

Shibobo collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only thing it saves is which Skool drills you've finished, and that stays on your own device.

1. Who is responsible

Shibobo is published by Okuhle Vezi, an individual developer in South Africa. For the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA), Okuhle Vezi is the responsible party for Shibobo and acts as its Information Officer. You can reach us at ovezi@wamtechsystems.co.za.

2. What Shibobo collects

Nothing. Shibobo has no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or social-media code, and it sets no tracking cookies. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.

3. What stays on your device

Two things are stored, by your browser, on your own phone or computer:

  • The game itself. The first time you open it, your browser saves the game's files so it can play offline. These are the same files for everyone and say nothing about you.
  • Your Skool progress. A list of the drills you've completed, kept in your browser's local storage under the name shibobo.skool.v1, so the next drill is ready when you come back.

That's all. Shibobo never asks for, or stores, your name, phone number, email address, location, contacts or photos.

4. Phone features it uses

All of these work on the device only; none of them send anything anywhere.

  • Touch, for the on-screen controls.
  • A game controller, if you pair one: the game reads its buttons and sticks while you play.
  • Vibration, a tiny tick when you press an on-screen button or flick a skill, where the phone supports it.
  • Screen wake lock, full screen and landscape, so the screen stays on and sideways during a match.

Shibobo does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

5. The map

The pitch on Bophirima Avenue and the streets and houses around it are built into the game from OpenStreetMap data before release. While you play, Shibobo does not contact OpenStreetMap or any other map service, and it does not know or ask where you are. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License.

6. Hosting and downloads (the web version)

The game and this website are hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When your device downloads the game (the first time, and later when it checks for a new version), GitHub's servers receive the technical details that every web request carries, such as your IP address and browser type, and may keep them in logs for security and operations under GitHub's privacy statement. We don't receive those logs and don't use them. GitHub's servers may be outside South Africa.

Your browser (for example Chrome or Safari) has its own privacy policy, which applies to how the browser itself works.

7. Google Play

The Android app on Google Play is the same game with all of its files packaged inside the app. It runs in your phone's built-in web view, plays without a connection, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. It asks only for Android's standard internet permission, which the game doesn't need to play. Google handles the download and your Google account under Google's own privacy policy, and gives us only summary statistics, such as install counts and crash and performance reports, which do not identify you.

8. Children

Shibobo is a sports game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

9. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask whether we hold personal information about you, ask to see it, and ask us to correct or delete it, and you may object to its processing. For Shibobo we hold none, and if you ask we will confirm that. If you are unhappy with how we handle a request, you can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa).

10. Deleting your data

Everything Shibobo keeps is on your device, so you can delete it yourself at any time:

  • Installed from the browser: remove Shibobo from your home screen, then clear the website data for okuvezi-dotcom.github.io in your browser's settings.
  • From Google Play: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Settings → Apps → Shibobo → Storage. Everything the app kept goes with it.

Step-by-step instructions for iPhone and Android are on the support page. Note that Predictly is served from the same web address, so clearing that site's data in your browser clears Predictly's saved data too.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at the top. If Shibobo ever starts sending any information off your device, this page will say so, and what and why, before that version is released.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy in Shibobo: ovezi@wamtechsystems.co.za.
